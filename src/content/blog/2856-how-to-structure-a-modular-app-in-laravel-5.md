Building large applications often necessitates modularity. Dividing your codebase into distinct modules allows for better separation of concerns, easier maintenance, and simpler scaling. When working with Laravel 5, achieving this structure requires careful planning around directory organization, namespaces, and how you interact with the framework's service container.

This guide will walk you through the best practices for structuring a modular application in Laravel 5, addressing your specific challenges regarding routing, service providers, and loading dependencies.
